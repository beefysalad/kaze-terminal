# Find in the terminal and docked tabs: verification

Branch `feat/find`, issue #155, for release 0.30, after 0.29 shipped (the version bump and changelog are their own PR).
Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-kaze-find.md`. Mockup: `docs/design/find-mockup.html`.

Built in three worktree lanes, merged in the order S, R, U.

## What shipped

Ctrl+Shift+F (Cmd+F on macOS) opens a find bar at the top right of the session's terminal or of a
workspace member's tab in the panel. Every match is tinted in the text, the current one stronger.
Marks on the pane's right edge show where all matches sit in the scrollback. Enter or Down steps to
the next match, Shift+Enter or Up to the previous, Aa switches to exact case, Esc closes and puts the
view back. See README, "Find".

- `a2afbbc` feat(find): Match a folded needle over a stream of cells (S1)

- `5e7662c` test(find): Benchmark full and incremental searches (S4)

Tests per task: `internal/find/match_test.go` (S1 to S3) and `internal/find/bench_test.go` (S4).

Benchmarks (S4; `go test -run XXX -bench . -benchmem ./internal/find/`, count 5, on the owner's
i7-1255U laptop, which is noisy, so ranges). A 200 x 50 screen, log-like lines of 120 to 200 columns,
the needle "warn":

| Benchmark | Time | Work | Allocations |
|---|---|---|---|
| FullScan10k (the default history) | 2.6 to 5.2 ms | 1.21M cells, 1.75 steps | 0 |
| FullScan100k | 25 to 49 ms | 11.95M cells, 12.8 steps | 0 |
| StepBudget (one step) | 2.0 to 2.5 ms | 1.00M cells | 0 |
| OutputRefresh (one new line, settled 10k search) | 56 to 65 us | 10.5k cells | 0 |

Final `StepBudget` is **1,000,000 cells** (`find.StepBudget`). It started at 2,000,000, but one such
step took 7.0 to 7.8 ms, over the 5 ms a frame may spend. At 1,000,000 a step takes 2.0 to 2.5 ms,
and about 4.3 ms at the slowest rate seen (4.3 ns a cell). The scan skips cells that cannot start a
match without touching the matcher, which took it from about 6 to 7 ns a cell down to 2 to 4 ns.

## Rulings made while planning

The 25 rulings are in the plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-04-kaze-find.md`, "Rulings made while
planning"). In short:

1. New package `internal/find`, over `internal/screen` only.
2. Stable line numbers: `Evicted()` plus the row.
3. Case-insensitive by default with simple lowercase folding; Aa for exact; no regex.
4. Matches do not overlap.
5. What counts as text (wide characters once, soft-wrapped rows run on, a hard line end breaks a match).
6. A full-screen app's screen is searched alone.
7. Bounded steps under the session lock, no goroutine.
8. New output rescans only what can change.
9. At most 1,000 matches, the newest kept.
10. Typing goes to the bar while it has the keyboard; a click in the terminal gives it back.
11. How the current match is chosen and when the view moves.
12. Find opens on the session or the shown member tab; elsewhere the shortcut does nothing.
13. One bar at a time, text kept for the window's life.
14. Scroll marks in the right margin.
15. Marks cached in their own ops.
16. Selection over current match over match over the cell's own colours.
17. Copy and paste in the bar.
18. Three new palette tokens.
19. No new `[colors]` keys.
20. The bar reuses chrome tokens; no shadow.
21. Placement, and what hides in a narrow pane.
22. No F3 or Cmd+G in this version.
23. Accessibility labels.
24. Nothing in bench mode changes.
25. Lanes share no file.

Changed during the build:

- **Ruling 7:** `StepBudget` lowered from 2,000,000 to 1,000,000 cells (S4, numbers above).
- **Ruling 18:** Midnight's match colour was moved to a blue tint apart from the selection, approved by
  the owner on 2026-11-04 (`dc926fc`). The rule that a match stays 60 or more colour units from the
  selection now holds in every theme, Midnight included.
- Rulings 3, 9 and 22 are unchanged. They are listed as known gaps in `docs/HANDOFF.md`.

No other ruling was changed by the owner.

## Review

Review: a fresh whole-branch review found two important focus bugs and one minor staleness bug. They were fixed test first in this commit range. (1) A member tab or the agent panel given the keyboard did not take it from the find bar, so typing meant for the member went into the find field: TestFindKeysLeaveWhenSidebarMemberClicked and TestFindKeysLeaveWhenPanelBodyClicked. (2) Ctrl+Shift+F from the sidebar filter, rename or branch launcher fields opened the bar but left the keyboard in the sidebar: TestFindFromSidebarFilterTakesKeyboard. (3) Opening or resizing the agent panel resized the session apart from the shared grid, so matches stayed stale until the next output: TestFindPanelResizeSearchesAgain, fixed with a lock-free resize counter on the session, so the still frame stays free of locks and allocations.

Left as is: while a long scan settles there is no current match ("0 of N", Enter does nothing), about 2 frames for 10k lines and about 20 for 100k. Esc gives the keyboard to the session, not back to the agent panel.
