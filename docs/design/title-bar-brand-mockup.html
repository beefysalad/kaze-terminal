Show the app name in the title bar

Windows apps like Calculator show their icon and name at the top left. kaze shows only its mark; the first “kaze” in the bar today is your repo folder, not the app.

Proposal: put the name Kaze next to the mark on Windows, and add the mark and name after the traffic lights on macOS.
