Try it

  • Fire alerts with the buttons above. Each session gets one card; a new alert from it replaces its card. A fourth card drops the oldest.
  • Click a card (or "Open session") to go there. The card and the sidebar dot clear.
  • × closes the card but keeps the dot: you haven't seen it yet.
  • Ctrl Shift J opens the newest card's session (click the page first).
  • "Done" cards hide by themselves after your done_toast time (8s here). "Needs you" stays until you visit.
  • Switch to Today's toast to compare, and try Washi for the light theme.

What's on the card

  • Mark: Claude, Codex or the workspace, with a badge: ! needs you, ✓ done.
  • Context line: branch and the slash command, e.g. ⎇ feature/V3-3149 · /review-pr.
  • Title: the session's sidebar name, so no more "zsh needs you".
  • Body: two lines, cut with "…". For "needs" it's Claude's own message. For "done" it's the start of Claude's last reply.
  • Status: "Needs your permission" / "Waiting for your answer" / "Done · ran 6m 40s".
  • Footer: "Open session" and its shortcut.

Rules kept

  • At most 3 cards, one per session. No animation, so idle CPU stays at zero.
  • Flat fill, 1px line, rounded corners from the theme's toast colours, so all five themes work.
  • Bottom right, above the status bar, where toasts are today, newest nearest the corner. With an agent or member panel open, the cards sit just above it.

Decided for you (say if you disagree)

Shortcut: reuse Ctrl Shift J (Cmd J on Mac), which already jumps to the next session that needs you, instead of adding Warp's Ctrl+Shift+G. One less key to learn.
Done preview: today a "done" alert carries no text. Kaze would read Claude's last reply from the Stop hook (the same field subagents already use) and show its first two lines.
Workspace runs use the same card, with the workspace's mark and its summary.
