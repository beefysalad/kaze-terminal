How it works

Try it: click api, web or worker under Workspaces, an agent line under the session, a tab, the ×, Hide, Expand, the status bar's server dots, other sessions, and drag the line above the tabs.

One panel per session

  • Members and subagents open as tabs in one panel under the terminal. One shows at a time; they share one height.
  • Drag the line to any height. Kaze remembers it (today's agent_panel_height).
  • Each session keeps its own tabs. Switch away and back and they're still there.

Opening and closing

  • Click a member or agent: it opens as a tab, or switches to its tab.
  • Click the one already showing: the panel hides (tabs kept). Same as the agent line today.
  • × closes a tab. A member keeps running; an agent is untouched.
  • Hide folds the panel away; open any tab again to bring it back.
  • A member is docked in one session at a time: opening it elsewhere moves its tab.
  • Up to 6 tabs; a seventh replaces the oldest one that isn't showing.

Members

  • The tab is the member's live terminal, at the panel's size. Click it to type; the accent line shows who has the keyboard. Click Claude's terminal to take it back.
  • Its dot shows up, waiting or down. The server dots in the status bar open the first member that is down.
  • Expand makes the member the main view, as today.

Removed

  • Scratch shell: the key, the title bar button and its hidden tabs. Claude Code's ! and Ctrl+Shift+N cover it.
  • Floating panes: dragging, resizing and stacking windows over the terminal.
  • An old scratch_shell key in config.toml is ignored with a warning.
