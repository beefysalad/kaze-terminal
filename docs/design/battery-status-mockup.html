Battery in the status bar

Shown in kaze's real bottom bar in four states: normal, charging, low (20% or less, amber) and very low (10% or less, red). On a desktop with no battery it's simply not there. Chosen look: option A (battery icon + percent), 2026-11-02. Not scheduled yet.

Behaviour: updated when the OS reports a power change (macOS power-source notification, Windows power-setting notification), or at most every 30 to 60 s on the existing status-bar refresh. No new timer, paused when kaze is hidden, redrawn only when the value changes, shown only on machines with a battery, dropped first when the window is too narrow.
