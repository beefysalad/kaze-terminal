Changelog.

Features, improvements and fixes, release by release. Kaze checks for updates in Settings, Updates.

  1. 0.35LatestNov 7, 2026

    A new app icon and logo

    • A new app icon: Kaze’s icon is now the refreshed wind K in soft lilac on a dark tile, in the Dock, Finder, the Windows taskbar and Alt+Tab. On macOS it follows Apple’s icon shape, so it sits the same size as your other apps. macOS may keep the old icon in the Dock until Kaze restarts after the update.
    • The same K everywhere: the settings sidebar now shows the new K too, matching the title bar and the About page.
  2. 0.34Nov 6, 2026

    A simpler sidebar

    • A simpler sidebar: the “New session on a branch” launcher is gone, with its shortcut and its worktree cleanup. Claude and Codex make branches and worktrees themselves, and the + in the title bar still opens a new session. The session list now runs down to your workspaces. Your existing worktrees and config are left as they are.
  3. 0.33Nov 6, 2026

    The Kaze name in the title bar, and correct app details on Windows

    • Kaze in the title bar: the lilac K and the name Kaze now sit at the top left of the window, the way Windows apps show their icon and name. On a Mac they follow the traffic lights, which had no mark before. In a narrow window the name steps aside first.
    • Correct app details on Windows: kaze.exe's Properties, Task Manager and security tools now show Kaze 0.33 with its name and publisher, instead of an old 0.6.
  4. 0.32Nov 5, 2026

    Five more themes, and one font everywhere

    • Five more themes: Matcha, Kōyō, Hai and Fuji are dark, Kiri is light, so there are ten in all. Pick one in Settings, Appearance; the cards sit five to a row, fewer in a narrow window.
    • One font everywhere: Settings, the palette, alert cards, the Agents board, docs and the title and status bars now use JetBrains Mono, the same font as the terminal, so the whole window reads as one. Kaze is a little smaller too.
  5. 0.31Nov 5, 2026

    A cleaner palette, and shortcuts you can change

    • A cleaner palette: it opens on what’s waiting on you, your sessions and the 3 skills you used last, with one row to browse the rest. Servers are no longer listed (they’re in the status bar and sidebar), and Kaze actions show up as you type their name.
    • Skills behind /: press / or Tab, or click Skills, to see every skill grouped as Yours, This project and one group per plugin. Type after the / to search by name or by what a skill does.
    • Edit your shortcuts: in Settings, Shortcuts, click a shortcut and press the new keys. It works at once. Kaze asks before taking keys another action uses, warns when a key belongs to your shell, and each row can be reset.
  6. 0.30Nov 5, 2026

    Find in the terminal

    • Find in the terminal: press Ctrl+Shift+F on Windows or Cmd+F on a Mac to search the whole scrollback of Claude’s terminal or a server tab in the panel. Every match is highlighted and marked on the right edge. Enter and Shift+Enter step through them, Aa matches case, and Esc closes the bar and puts the view back where it was.
    • Built to stay out of the way: new output updates the count without moving what you’re reading, a click in the terminal gives you the keyboard back, plain Ctrl+F still goes to the shell, and nothing runs while the bar is closed.
  7. 0.29Nov 4, 2026

    The battery on macOS, and a new About page

    • Battery on macOS: MacBooks now show the battery at the far right of the status bar too, just like Windows laptops: the level and percent, a bolt while charging, amber at 20% and red at 10%. It updates when macOS says the level or the charger changed, so it costs nothing while idle. Mac desktops show nothing.
    • A new About page: Settings, About is now a compact page built around the refreshed logo, with the tagline, what the name means, and one card for the version, update status, platform and license. Copy build info, View on GitHub and Report an issue sit right under them, and stack neatly in a narrow window.
  8. 0.28.2Nov 4, 2026

    Markdown links that find their file

    • Fixed: links to files Claude wrote elsewhere: clicking a .md link that Claude printed now opens the file Claude actually wrote or read, even when it lives outside the tab's folder, like a note in your Obsidian vault or a file in another repo. Before, Kaze only looked in the tab's folder and said it couldn't find the file.
  9. 0.28.1Nov 4, 2026

    A new home for Kaze

    • Kaze has a new home: the project moved to beefysalad/kaze-terminal on GitHub. Kaze now checks there for updates, and the website is at beefysalad.github.io/kaze-terminal. Nothing else changes.
  10. 0.28Nov 4, 2026

    Readable agent steps, and the battery on Windows

    • Read what your agents are doing: a subagent’s panel and the agents board now read like Claude’s own transcript: the agent’s words first, then one sentence per step, like “Edited routes.ts +8 −5”, “Read 3 files” or “Ran the tests · failed · 1 test”. Click a step to see its diff or output. “Every step” brings back the full list.
    • Battery in the status bar: on Windows laptops, the battery level sits at the far right of the status bar, with a bolt while charging, amber at 20% and red at 10%. It updates when Windows says it changed, so it costs nothing while idle. Desktops show nothing. macOS comes later.
    • Fixed: swapped agent names: subagents of the same type started together keep their own task names instead of trading them.
  11. 0.27Nov 4, 2026

    Servers docked under the terminal, and alerts you can read

    • Servers in the panel: workspace members open as tabs in the panel under the terminal, next to your subagents, instead of floating over Claude. Click a member’s tab to type in it, click the terminal to take the keyboard back, and use Expand to make it the main view.
    • Richer alert cards: a card at the bottom right names the session the way the sidebar does, shows its branch and slash command, Claude’s message or the start of its last reply, and how long the turn ran. Ctrl+Shift+J (⌘ J on macOS) opens the newest one.
    • Links with spaces: a .md path with a space in it, or one printed after its folder, is underlined whole and opens on Ctrl+click. When it can’t be found, Kaze says where it looked.
    • Removed: the scratch shell and floating panes: the docked panel replaces both. An old scratch_shell line in config.toml is ignored with a warning.
  12. 0.26Nov 3, 2026

    An agents board you can work from

    • A new agents board: filter by All, Needs you, Running or Done, and search by type, task, session or repo (press /). Waiting agents sit in a pinned Needs you card; the rest are grouped under their session, with a bar showing what’s waiting, running and done. Finished agents fold away.
    • Preview without leaving: pick an agent to see its steps and messages, or its formatted report, beside the list. Double-click it, press Enter or use Open in session to jump to it.
    • One click opens an agent: clicking an agent line in the sidebar opens its panel again, and a second click closes it. “+N more” opens the board for that session.
    • A quieter highlight: the project rail is gone. The project you’re in keeps its bold name and your session keeps its own bar.
    • Fixed: report summaries: a finished agent shows the first sentence of its report, not its heading.
  13. 0.25Nov 3, 2026

    A calmer sidebar and colour emoji

    • A tidier agent tray: each subagent takes one line under its session. At most three show, waiting first; the rest sit behind “+N more”, and “N agents” folds the whole tray. Hover a line to see what the agent is doing.
    • Safe clicks: clicking an agent line shows its session, so a stray click lands on your terminal. Open an agent’s panel with the › at the end of the line, or a double-click.
    • Find your project: a thin accent rail marks the project you’re in, instead of a tinted box. Sidebar text stays readable in every theme.
    • Colour emoji: ✅ ❌ 🔄 ⏳ and friends draw in full colour on Windows and macOS, like your other terminals. Each one is drawn once and reused.
    • Fixed: title symbols: Claude’s status symbols no longer show as an empty box in the title bar or the sidebar.
  14. 0.24Nov 3, 2026

    Follow every subagent, copy and paste on Windows

    • Agent panel: click a subagent to open its panel under the terminal instead of a floating card. Activity lists each step with the agent’s own messages between them; Report shows the finished report. Drag the edge to any height, and each session remembers its panel.
    • Formatted reports: bold, code, lists, headings and tables render in the panel. Copy report copies the original markdown, and Open in reader shows it in the doc reader.
    • Copy and paste on Windows: Ctrl+C copies a selection and otherwise still interrupts; Ctrl+V pastes text and screenshots. Typing clears an old selection, so Ctrl+C always stops Claude after you type.
    • Fixed: workspace setup tabs: a member whose commands finished reads Ready instead of stopped, and the workspace header no longer counts it as down.
  15. 0.23Nov 3, 2026

    Keep your place, start another session

    • Active project: a soft accent tint highlights the current repo header alongside its selected session. It stays visible through Settings, documents, floating panes and folded groups.
    • Clear running marks: a still dot inside a closed ring replaces the frozen-looking spinner in session rows, subagents, the title bar and the agents pill. No animation frames.
    • Kaze stays open: closing the final session leaves a New session button and your shortcut ready. Settings, Workspaces and cleanup dialogs stay available.
    • Shift+Enter: add a new line in Claude Code without sending. Plain Enter still submits; Alt/Option+Enter continues to work.
  16. 0.22Nov 2, 2026

    A clearer sidebar, a session for every branch

    • Two-line session rows: agent logos, useful names and status above the branch or worktree and line counts. Repo headers keep the count without repeating state dots.
    • Start on a branch: choose a repo, branch and Claude, Codex or Shell. Kaze reuses the branch’s worktree or creates a sibling folder, then waits for the shell before starting the agent. Open with Cmd+Shift+N on macOS or Ctrl+Shift+N on Windows; tuck the launcher away with one click.
    • Worktree cleanup: Kaze remembers only the worktrees it creates and asks before removing clean or finished ones. Branches stay, and Git refuses folders with uncommitted or untracked changes.
    • Cheaper redraws: unchanged sidebar frames reuse their model and drawing, without allocating or reshaping session rows.
  17. 0.21Nov 2, 2026

    Read docs and follow subagent conversations

    • Markdown reader: Cmd+click an .md path (Ctrl+click on Windows) to read it in Kaze, with an outline, links and automatic reloads. Each doc gets a temporary row under its session.
    • Live Claude subagent messages: panes show assistant messages as they arrive, with tool calls folded into count summaries. Scroll up to keep your place, or follow new output at the bottom.
    • Fixed: empty reports: subagent panes recover reports delivered through SubagentHandback. Copy report copies the recovered text; a missing report says “No report”. Pane links and markdown paths open with Cmd+click or Ctrl+click.
    • Cleaner resource card: session names drop leading agent status symbols, and the CPU/RAM card has a short, single-line footer.
  18. 0.20Nov 2, 2026

    Resource usage in the status bar

    • CPU and RAM in the status bar: the status bar shows how much CPU and memory Kaze and the shells, agents and servers it runs are using. Hover it for a breakdown by session. It refreshes on activity, at most every 2 seconds, and pauses while Kaze is minimized.
    • Fixed: resumed conversations: after an update or a launch from Finder, restored tabs resume their Claude Code or Codex conversation again, including when the agent is installed as a shell alias.
    • Fixed: update prompt: the update confirmation text no longer overlaps the Cancel and Update now buttons.
    • Fixed: workspace status: a closed workspace tab shows as stopped and no longer counts as a running server. The count reads N/M UP.
  19. 0.19.1Nov 1, 2026

    Session shortcuts and smoother subagent panes

    • macOS session switching: Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab now reach Kaze and switch sessions again, including while a scratch pane is focused. Custom bindings still work.
    • Smoother subagent panes: long prompts and reports wrap with much less work. Reopened helpers start a fresh turn, and panes show the latest report even when it is the same length as the previous one.
    • Responsive sessions: Git folder and branch lookups run in the background. Input queues have a byte limit and show a notice if a busy terminal cannot accept more input.
  20. 0.19Nov 1, 2026

    Subagent conversations and a start folder

    • Subagents read like a conversation: click a subagent to see the prompt it was given, one line on what it has done so far ("Searched for 13 patterns, ran 15 shell commands") and, when it finishes, its report. Individual tool calls are no longer listed. Works for Claude Code subagents and Codex helpers.
    • Start folder: choose where new tabs open in Settings, Workspaces, Startup (Choose… or Use home), or set start_dir in the config. Workspaces and restored sessions keep their own folders, and a folder that has gone missing falls back to home with a warning.
  21. 0.18Nov 1, 2026

    Your sessions come back

    • Automatic restoration: reopen Kaze and your sessions return with their folders, names, order and selected session, including after an update.
    • Resume the same conversation: configured Claude Code and Codex hooks identify each chat, so conversations in the same project stay separate. Resuming waits for shell readiness and never resubmits your prompt.
    • Your choice: turn restoration off in Settings, Workspaces, Startup. Workspace recipes are not replayed; running commands and servers still stop when Kaze closes.
  22. 0.17.1Nov 1, 2026

    Codex logo, no setup

    • Fixed: the Codex logo now shows for interactive Codex. Kaze sees Claude Code or Codex running in a tab by itself and shows its logo within about a second, before your first prompt, with no hooks needed (macOS).
  23. 0.17Nov 1, 2026

    Agent logos and colored links

    • Agent logos in the sidebar: a session running Claude Code shows the Claude logo, and one running Codex shows the OpenAI logo, next to the agent's name.
    • Named as soon as it opens: the logo and name appear the moment Claude Code or Codex starts, before your first prompt. Settings, Agents offers the new SessionStart hook for each; in Codex, run /hooks to trust it.
    • Links stand out: web links in the terminal are drawn in blue with an underline. Text a program already colored keeps its color and gets the underline.
  24. 0.16Nov 1, 2026

    Clickable links and Codex helpers

    • Clickable links: web links in the terminal show a hand cursor. ⌘-click them (Ctrl-click on Windows) to open them in your browser, even while Claude Code is running. A plain click still selects text.
    • Codex helpers: the helpers Codex starts now show in the sidebar, the live panes and the agents board, just like Claude Code subagents. Settings, Agents, Codex offers the three new hooks; then run /hooks in Codex to trust them.
    • Clear finished subagents: each done card on the agents board has a × to remove it, and Clear empties the Done column.
    • Paste screenshots with ⌘ V: with an image on the clipboard, ⌘ V pastes it into Claude Code or Codex. A plain shell is left alone.
    • Fixed: subagents started without a type show their task again, and Claude Code's own background helpers no longer appear as "subagent" cards.
    • Fixed: sessions in the same repo stay in one sidebar group, even when the folder was typed with different capitals.
    • Fixed: the running ring in the title bar is no longer cut in half.
  25. 0.15.1Nov 1, 2026

    Smoother sessions, less overhead

    • Sidebar fixes: toggling the sidebar no longer flashes, and finished subagents leave its tree once none remain running. Their reports stay on the agents board.
    • Shorter title-bar commands: long plugin commands show a compact name, while the sidebar and palette keep the full name.
    • Faster busy terminals: scrolling reuses rows, and unchanged title-bar and status-bar layouts are reused instead of measured again each frame.
    • Less memory churn: subagent history and rendering buffers reuse storage, and forgotten subagent pane IDs are cleaned up.
    • More efficient agent updates: bursts of tool activity are compacted in batches, protecting completion reports and the newest actions.
  26. 0.15Nov 1, 2026

    A new title bar and status bar

    • One title bar on Mac and Windows: the sidebar toggle, a new-session button, the session you're in with its command and state, a search field that opens the palette, and the scratch shell, Agents and Settings buttons, laid out the same on both.
    • Real Mac window buttons: the native traffic lights sit inside Kaze's own bar. Drag the bar to move the window; double-click it to zoom, as your Mac is set.
    • A clearer status bar: folder and branch with icons, pills for updates and subagents, server dots, and Claude usage as small meters with a card that shows when each limit resets.
    • Fits narrow windows: the least important parts give way first, so nothing overlaps.
    • Fixed: the agents board now names sessions the same way as the sidebar.
  27. 0.14Oct 31, 2026

    See what your subagents are doing

    • Subagents in the sidebar: each session lists its subagents with their task, how long they’ve run and the step they’re on.
    • A live pane per subagent: click one to follow its steps as they happen and read its report when it finishes, with a Copy report button.
    • The agents board: every subagent in every session, in running, waiting on you and done. Open it with the Agents button, the status bar, the palette or ⌘ ⇧ A (Ctrl Shift A on Windows).
    • Setup in one click: Settings, Agents checks the three new Claude Code hooks and Fix adds them, showing the change first.
    • Fixed: pressing Command on its own no longer types a ⌘ into the terminal.
  28. 0.13Oct 31, 2026

    Claude Code and Codex, side by side

    • Agents settings: shared alert controls, with separate setup checks for Claude Code and Codex.
    • Codex hooks: see when Codex needs you, is running or is done. Interrupting Codex clears its running state without a done alert.
    • Know which agent: session rows and the palette show Claude or Codex beside the command, including Codex’s $skill names.
    • Setup with a preview: Codex Fix asks before writing, backs up existing hooks, and leaves your other hooks and config alone. Then review and trust Kaze’s hooks with /hooks in Codex.
    • A refreshed lander: native Kaze 0.12 screenshots, an Appearance view in the gallery, and a mobile layout with roomier navigation and buttons.
  29. 0.12Oct 31, 2026

    A redesigned settings page

    • A new look: settings sit in cards, with a clean UI font and new controls. Monospace is kept for keys, paths and commands.
    • Appearance: theme cards that preview each theme, a live preview of your theme, font size and cursor, and cursor shapes you can see.
    • Workspaces: one card per workspace with its state, and each tab’s folder, commands and start condition at a glance.
    • Claude: your Claude Code setup first, with a Connected or Missing mark per hook and a one-click Fix.
    • Shortcuts: grouped, drawn as key caps, and searchable.
    • Updates: its own page, with the update status, progress while it downloads, and the automatic-check switch.
    • About: roomier, with your version, platform and a Report an issue button.
    • New icons for the sidebar, search, scratch shell and settings buttons, drawn as one matched set.
  30. 0.11Oct 31, 2026

    Downloads and updates

    • Download Kaze for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Windows, no Go needed.
    • Updates in place: Kaze checks GitHub once a day, shows “Update” in the status bar, and installs a new version with one click after asking. Each download is checked against the release’s checksums.
  31. 0.10

    Floating panes and the scratch shell

    • Floating panes: a workspace’s servers float over your Claude session. Drag, resize, type into them, or expand one to the main view.
    • Scratch shell: Ctrl ` opens a quick shell in the session’s folder; press it again to hide it.
  32. 0.9

    The palette

    • One key finds everything: ⌘ K lists waiting sessions, every session and server, your Claude Code skills and Kaze’s own actions.
